Mask Detection

Deep Learning Real-time

🎯 Problem statement

During the COVID-19 pandemic, enforcing mask-wearing in public spaces required constant manual monitoring — a labor-intensive and error-prone process. This project builds a convolutional neural network (CNN) that detects in real time whether a person in a camera frame is wearing a mask correctly, incorrectly, or not at all.

About me

Medical AI Researcher & AI Engineer

I am a Machine Learning researcher and Python developer focused on applying AI to healthcare problems in South Asian clinical settings — a domain where global models consistently fail because local patient data is absent from their training.

My IEEE-accepted research on Antimicrobial Resistance (AMR) prediction addresses this gap directly. Alongside research, I build and deploy practical AI tools: CV screening systems, biomedical data pipelines, and LLM-powered automation — using PyTorch, HuggingFace, scikit-learn, Django, and the major LLM APIs.

Currently completing my BSc in Computer Science and Engineering at IIUC, I am actively pursuing postgraduate study in Medical AI while remaining available for freelance projects and research collaborations.

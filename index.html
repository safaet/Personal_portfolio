About me

Medical AI & Bengali NLP Researcher

I am a Machine Learning researcher and Python developer focused on two underserved problems: low-resource NLP for Bengali — a language with 230M+ speakers and almost no dedicated ML tooling — and medical AI for South Asian clinical settings, where global models consistently fail because local data is absent from their training.

On the Bengali NLP side, I fine-tune BanglaBERT for text classification, build bilingual Bengali-English multimodal classifiers, and lead a production Bengali-language RAG system as Technical Lead, AI/ML at Neuralis AI. On the medical AI side, my research on Antimicrobial Resistance (AMR) prediction, accepted at ECCT 2026 in collaboration with icddr,b, addresses the same local-data gap. I use PyTorch, HuggingFace, scikit-learn, Django, and the major LLM APIs across both.

Currently completing my BSc in Computer Science and Engineering at IIUC, I am actively pursuing postgraduate study in Medical AI while remaining available for freelance projects and research collaborations.
